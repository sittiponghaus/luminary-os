## Purpose

Personal Platform for Thai-way of organizing the wedding invitation in the modern way.

## Background

Everyone grown up and have relate with several peoples from every slice of life, some on first office, some on second or 10th. They're someone we feel like they're special to us and want to invited. But not everyone feel the same way, they need to know if they're being alone in those event of 100-1000 of unknown peoples or what. As a guest, they need some familiar to go and hang out together since the bridge / groom will have so much time together with them.

One meaningful moment here is to make sure they have familiar they can joined together as they also grown away and use this event to re-activate their relationship once again.

## Feature Idea

- Wedding Landing Page
- Personalized Invitation Card that allowed user to know who they'll see when they're in those event.
- Donation
- Chinese Banquet Allocation
